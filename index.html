A little about me

02 / 03

The story is always bigger than the headline.

I am a Bengaluru-based journalist on the Global News Monitoring desk at Thomson Reuters, covering the fast-moving stories shaping business and technology around the world. I am drawn to clear, rigorous reporting and to the human details that make complex stories matter.

My reporting has taken me from global corporate news to city policy, public spaces, culture and community. Alongside journalism, I hold a Master's in Multimedia Journalism and have worked in communications, corporate CSR partnerships, and campaign design for animal welfare and community organisations.
